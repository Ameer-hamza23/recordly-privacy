Recordly

Android recording demonstration

A 52-second demonstration of Recordly recording another app while its audio and camera controls are used.

Shown in the video

  • Start screen recording, then open a chess app.
  • Select microphone, game audio, both sources, or mute.
  • Enable the optional camera overlay during recording.
  • View the ongoing Android recording notification.

Real app footage with privacy edits. An illustrated sample character replaces the camera view, including its opening movement. This character is added during editing; it is not an in-app camera effect. Other personal details are obscured. English explanatory narration is added, and a short original microphone sample has its voice altered for privacy. No app actions are generated or reconstructed.
