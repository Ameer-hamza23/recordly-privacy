Privacy policy

Privacy policy for Recordly: Screen Recorder, the Android screen recording app operated by Zenith Stack Studio.

Effective 15 October 2026 · Package: com.recordly.screenrecorder

Recordly saves recordings and screenshots on your device. Its advertising and subscription integrations do not receive your screen recordings, microphone recordings, screenshots or camera images.

Recording and permissions

Recordly processes your screen and, when enabled, microphone audio, supported app audio and camera images to create the recording you request. Android asks for screen capture permission before a recording starts. You choose whether to use microphone audio or the camera overlay.

Files are saved through Android’s media storage, including Movies/Recordly for videos. Recording settings and library information are stored locally. Floating controls use Android’s display-over-other-apps permission; notifications provide recording status and controls. These permissions support the features you choose to use.

Recordly does not operate a cloud recording storage service. If you share a file using Android’s share menu, the destination app or service processes that file under its own privacy policy. Device backups and gallery synchronization are controlled by your device settings and other apps.

Advertising

The free version uses Google AdMob. Depending on your privacy choices and applicable requirements, Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address and approximate location, advertising and device identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. This supports ad delivery, measurement, analytics, security and fraud prevention.

Consent choices are requested where required. You can revisit available ad privacy choices in Recordly’s Settings and manage advertising identifiers in Android’s settings. Premium removes ads in Recordly. Advertising choices do not give Google access to the contents of your recordings.

Read Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Subscriptions and purchases

Google Play processes subscription payments. RevenueCat validates purchases and provides Premium access. These services process purchase history, transaction or purchase identifiers, subscription status, a RevenueCat app user identifier, and technical information needed to operate and secure purchases. Recordly does not receive your full payment card details.

Zenith Stack Studio may provide optional complimentary access codes. Redeeming a code connects Recordly to a separately provisioned RevenueCat customer identifier to check Premium access; it does not charge your Google Play account or create an email-based login. Keep codes private. You can disconnect a code in the Premium screen and contact us to request deletion of the associated record.

Monthly and yearly subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled through Google Play. The purchase screen shows the available plan, local price and billing period before you confirm. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play. Uninstalling Recordly does not cancel a subscription.

Read RevenueCat’s Privacy Policy. Google Play’s payment and subscription terms apply to purchases made there.

Deletion, retention and your choices

You can delete recordings and screenshots from Recordly’s Library or your device’s gallery or file manager. Removing the app may leave media in shared device storage; delete those files separately if you no longer want them. Clearing app data removes local settings. Copies shared or backed up to another service must be managed with that service.

Advertising and purchase providers retain information according to their own policies and legal or operational requirements. Contact us to request access to or deletion of subscription-related information associated with your use of Recordly. We may need information sufficient to identify the relevant purchase or app user record. Some transaction information may need to be retained for legal, accounting, security or fraud-prevention purposes.

Security and children’s privacy

Recordly relies on Android permissions and device storage protections. Google and RevenueCat use encrypted connections for their service communications. No storage or transmission method is completely secure.

Recordly is a general-purpose recording utility and is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly request children’s personal information. If you believe a child has provided personal information through Recordly’s services, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

Policy changes

We may update this policy when Recordly’s features, service providers or data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. Material changes will be communicated as required.
